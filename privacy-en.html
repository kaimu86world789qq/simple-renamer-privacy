Privacy Policy (Easy Sequential Renamer)

Last updated: May 19, 2026
Operator: Trierr (Japanese reading: トリエラ / “Toriera”)
This app is operated by an individual, not a corporation. “Trierr” is the trade name / brand name used for this app and related store listings.

This policy describes how information is handled in the mobile application “かんたん連番リネーム” (English name: Easy Sequential Renamer, the “App”).

1. What the App does

The App batch-renames image files (jpg / jpeg / png / webp) in a folder you choose on your device to sequential file names. As implemented, the App does not upload image contents or file lists to servers operated by the operator.

2. Information stored on your device (not sent to the operator’s servers)

The App may store the following locally on your device for functionality. It is not designed for the operator to remotely access or view this data.

CategoryPurpose (as implemented)
Rename settings Base name, start number, digit count (restored in the form on next launch)
Last folder used Folder identifier (URI) and display name (“Open last folder”)
Ad pacing Launch count for interstitials, timestamp of last interstitial, 24-hour cooldown
Undo (restore previous names) Previous and new file names and URIs for the last rename only (in memory; cleared when the App exits)

Folder URIs and file names may reveal information about your device environment; unless such data leaves the device, the operator does not receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads on screen and, under certain conditions, interstitial (full-screen) ads.

For ads, identifiers and device/interaction-related data may be shared with Google and its partners and used for personalized ads, measurement, and related purposes. See Google’s policies for details.

The current App build does not integrate the User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. It may be added in the future if required by law or Google policies.

4. Files and storage access

The App uses Android’s Storage Access Framework (SAF) and related OS mechanisms so you can list and rename files in folders you explicitly select. File contents and name lists are not sent to the operator’s servers as implemented.

5. Image preview

Thumbnails and full-screen preview load images on your device. Temporary cache files may be created locally; they are not uploaded to the operator’s servers.

6. Analytics / crash SDKs (dedicated)

The App’s dependencies do not include a separate first-party analytics SDK or dedicated crash reporting SDK (e.g. Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics) in the source tree. (Network activity from Google’s advertising SDKs is described above.)

7. Third parties

For advertising, data may be shared with Google LLC, its affiliates, and advertising partners as described in Google’s policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Retention and deletion

Local data can often be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing app storage (varies by OS). Undo data is held in memory only and is not kept after the App is closed. For ad-related data, follow Google’s settings and help.

9. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13. If minors use the App, parental guidance is recommended.

10. International transfers

Advertising-related processing may involve transfer and processing outside your country (e.g. including the United States or the EEA). See Google’s policies.

This policy may change with laws or App features. The posted version applies. Material changes may be communicated in the App where appropriate.
